About Hyperlink

The ICT and multimedia backbone of Nazarene Catholic School — a self-sustaining technical alliance built by students, for students and faculty alike.

Our Mission Meet the Team
Community Focus

TARGET AUDIENCE: TEACHERS & STUDENTS

The Hyperlink Club is specifically structured to support two vital pillars of the Nazarene Catholic School community: our primary/secondary student body and our dedicated teaching faculty. In an era where digital literacy is paramount, students face the challenge of transitioning from passive tech-consumers to analytical problem solvers. Similarly, faculty members require consistent, high-level multimedia support to translate complex curriculum goals into engaging classroom presentations, digital modules, and event documentation.

Our Vision

OUR MISSION & VISION

Founded on the principles of academic excellence, service, and digital innovation, our mission is to establish a self-sustaining technical alliance across campus. We streamline how the club handles:

  1. Multimedia Production: Crafting high-impact presentation decks and professional video editing.
  2. Event Photography: Unedited, high-fidelity visual documentation of campus milestones.
  3. Publicity Materials (Pubmats): Clear, engaging visual communication and announcement flyers.
  4. School System Support: Assisting faculty and staff with institutional digital infrastructure.
